In the with_caregiver subgroup, we further compared early-window text representations against the complete visit-level caregiver text (`full_text`). For early-window analysis, we evaluated the first 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, and 15 caregiver turns. Classification performance remained relatively stable across settings, with several early windows (especially 6 and 8 turns) reaching high classification scores, suggesting that a short early segment may already contain substantial screening information. However, for continuous score prediction, `full_text` showed the strongest overall regression performance, achieving the highest correlation with both GAD-7 (r = 0.301) and PHQ-9 (r = 0.361), alongside lower prediction error than most truncated windows. These results suggest that early dialogue windows may be sufficient for coarse risk classification, whereas complete visit-level caregiver text provides a more informative representation for modeling the continuous severity of anxiety and depression symptoms.
